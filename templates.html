The template library

Seven architectures for 25–30 residues

Every template keeps one metal site (never tandem), caps that are basic or His, and acid confined to the binding region at D/E ≤ 6 — except T7, which breaks only the acid ceiling it exists to test. T2 is the one to back: a cationic delivery domain held away from the metal site, following the finding that the programme is delivery-limited. T1 and T4 raise denticity 2 → 6 with imidazole, not carboxylate — more donors, no more acid.

Length is a client override; above ~22 aa the acid ceiling and measured chemistry cannot both hold — these are hypotheses to test, not predictions

If asked why go long at all: our best peptide holds Fe3+ with only two donors, while siderophore-class chelators are hexadentate at log K 24 to 26. Raising denticity needs chain length. That is a better answer than "the client asked for it", and it is the honest one.
